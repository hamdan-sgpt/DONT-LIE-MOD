Interactive Gameplay Flowchart & System Diagram

flowchart TD
    Start(["🚀 Game Setup & Start"]) --> RoleAssign["🎴 Role Assignment
(Mafia, Doctor, Police, Citizens)"]
    RoleAssign --> GiveMoney["💰 Mafia menerima Money Pouch"]
    
    GiveMoney --> HidingPhase["🙈 Hiding Phase (1-2 Min)
Warga ditutup matanya, Mafia menyembunyikan Money Pouch di Chest"]
    
    HidingPhase --> SearchPhase["🔍 Search Phase (3-5 Min)
Pintu terbuka, Warga menggeledah ruangan & Mafia mengecoh"]
    
    SearchPhase --> CheckFound{"Apakah Money Pouch
ditemukan Warga?"}
    CheckFound -- "Ya" --> KeepSecret["Warga menyimpan Money Pouch secara rahasia"]
    CheckFound -- "Belum" --> Discussion["🗣️ Discussion Phase"]
    KeepSecret --> Discussion
    
    Discussion --> DiscussionDetail["Pemain berkumpul di Meja Rapat & berdebat adu alibi"]
    DiscussionDetail --> Voting["🗳️ Voting Phase (45 Detik)"]
    
    Voting --> EliminatePlayer["❌ Pemain dengan vote terbanyak DIELIMINASI"]
    
    EliminatePlayer --> CheckWinCond1{"Apakah SELURUH Mafia
berhasil dieliminasi?"}
    
    CheckWinCond1 -- "Ya" --> CheckMoneyCond{"Apakah Money Pouch
dipegang/ditemukan Warga?"}
    CheckMoneyCond -- "Ya" --> CitizenWin(["🟢 KEMENANGAN TIM WARGA (CITIZENS)!"])
    CheckMoneyCond -- "Tidak" --> MafiaWin(["🔴 KEMENANGAN TIM MAFIA!"])
    
    CheckWinCond1 -- "Tidak (Mafia Masih Ada)" --> NightPhase["🌙 Night Phase (45 Detik)
Layar Gelap & Slowness"]
    
    NightPhase --> NightActions["⚡ Aksi Malam:
- 🗡️ Mafia: Membunuh 1 Korban
- 🩺 Doctor: Melindungi 1 Pemain
- 🔍 Police: Cek Identitas 1 Pemain"]
    
    NightActions --> MorningAnnounce["☀️ Morning Announcement
Pengumuman Korban Selamat / Tewas"]
    
    MorningAnnounce --> CheckWinCond2{"Apakah Jumlah Mafia >= Warga
ATAU Waktu Habis?"}
    
    CheckWinCond2 -- "Ya" --> MafiaWin
    CheckWinCond2 -- "Tidak" --> SearchPhase
